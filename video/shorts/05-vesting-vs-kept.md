# Short 05: Vesting vs KEPT

## The prompt

A 20 second 16:9 film (1920x1080, 30 fps) in the launch trailer's style: one 3D world split down
the middle by a wall of light. On the left, vesting: a calendar unlocks a quarter of the team's
tokens every three months and nothing ships. On the right, KEPT: the swarm checks each milestone,
three are kept and pay out, one is missed and burns. Both run at once, side by side, on the beat.
The camera flies forward through the year, cranes up over both lanes for the result, then lands
on the logo.

## Why it works

- Split screens compare at a glance; the viewer reads both sides without being told what to read.
- The same four beats on both sides make the difference impossible to miss: left always pays,
  right pays only for proof.
- It ends on a line people repeat: "Time isn't proof. Delivery is."
