# 03: KEPT launch trailer

## The prompt

A 30 second 16:9 trailer (1920x1080, 30 fps) that combines a particle-heavy cinematic trailer with
a product-launch film. The particles are the IMD swarm: they draw every card, carry every
transition, and become the logo. The cards are glass recreations of KEPT's real screens, so the
film proves a working product, not a mood. No narration, few words, every change on a beat.

## Look

- Particles: 6,000 swarm points and 2,400 dust points in real 3D (three.js), with a custom shader
  for a sharp core, a soft halo and depth-of-field blur, plus a quarter-resolution blurred copy
  screened on top for bloom.
- Cards: dark glass, a 1.5 px edge lit in the verdict colour, deep shadows. They materialise out of
  the particles that draw their outline and dissolve back into them.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans Light for the lines, Plex Mono for numbers and labels.
- Finish: film grain, vignette, a white flash on every big hit, a slow camera drift with pushes
  on the reveal, the drop and the logo.
- Colour: black, white light, verdict green `#3ecf7a`, alarm red `#ff6b62`, gold for tokens.

## Sound

120 bpm in D minor. A drone and a lone bell, a pulse that builds, a shatter into silence, a reverse
swell into a braam on the reveal, a sidechained supersaw groove, a snare roll into the drop, a
rising bell for each vote, poured-gold grains for the payout, embers for the burn, half-time hits
on the numbers, a warm breath on the line, and a final braam on the logo.
