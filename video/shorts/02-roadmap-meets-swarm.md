# Short 02: POV, your roadmap meets the swarm

## The prompt

A 20 second square video (1080x1080, 30 fps) for X. A team's roadmap gets checked line by line by
the IMD swarm. Two promises are kept and pay out; two are broken and burn. It ends on a question
the viewer answers in the replies: would your roadmap survive? No narration; it reads muted.

## Why it works

- "POV" is a format people already scroll for, and everyone has watched a roadmap slip.
- Four quick verdicts in a row: the viewer guesses each one before the robots answer.
- It shows the whole product in 20 seconds: milestones, the vote, release or burn.
- The closing question invites replies, and replies spread the post.

## Look

The cast from the explainer on the near-black stage: the roadmap poster on its pin, the team
blob in a hard hat, nine robot agents in a 3x3 grid, gold coins, a small fire for broken promises.
Kept turns a line green with a check; broken turns it red with a cross. IBM Plex Mono throughout.

## Sound

A swaggering groove while the team shows off its roadmap, a tense pulse while the swarm checks, a
rising ding for every kept line and a buzzer plus fire crackle for every broken one, then a punchy
hit on the scorecard and a held chord on the end card.
