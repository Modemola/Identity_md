# Short 01: Imdelivering

## The prompt

A 20 second square video (1080x1080, 30 fps) for X that answers the IMD founder's "Im-" word
list with the one word that is KEPT: **Imdelivering**. No narration: it reads with the sound
off and lands harder with it on.

## Why it works

- It rides a post the IMD community already knows, so IMD people get the joke in one frame.
- The same pace as the founder's list for 5 seconds, then a hard stop that the eye cannot skip.
- The payoff is the product itself: the swarm votes, the stamp lands, the word turns green.

## Look

The explainer's cast and palette: near-black stage, verdict green `#3ecf7a`, gold coins, the
nine robot agents, the team blob in a hard hat. IBM Plex Mono, huge. "Im" stays fixed in white;
the rest of the word swaps on every beat like a slot machine reel.

## Sound

112 bpm, C major, kick and clap from the first frame. A tick on every word swap. A record
scratch and a dead stop at the question. Beeps while the robots check, a rising ding per vote, a
stamp thump, a party popper and coin clinks on the payoff, then the end card on a held chord.
