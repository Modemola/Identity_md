# KEPT explainer: creative brief

## The ask

A two minute animated explainer for X that makes people stop scrolling and watch to the end. It
explains the problem KEPT solves, why it matters, and how KEPT fixes it, so clearly that someone
who has never heard of IMD or vesting understands it on the first watch, with the sound off.

## Audience

Crypto Twitter: token holders, builders, hackathon judges. They have seen a hundred roadmaps that
never shipped. They scroll fast and watch muted.

## Tone

Playful, confident, a little cheeky. A cartoon, not a pitch deck. Never preachy, never accusing a
real project. The joke is on "coming soon", not on anyone in particular.

## Look

- A cast of characters, drawn in code as flat vector shapes with squash, stretch and overshoot:
  - **Holders**: round blobs with big eyes that cheer, wait, doze off and frown.
  - **The team**: a blob in a hard hat.
  - **Tokens**: gold coins stamped with K.
  - **The vault**: a chunky safe with the KEPT seal.
  - **The swarm**: nine small robot agents with a single glowing eye.
- The KEPT palette on a dark stage, with more colour than the product: verdict green
  `#3ecf7a`, alarm red `#ff6b62`, gold `#f0b429`, white ink, and a soft coloured spotlight per
  scene. IBM Plex Mono for every word.
- Motion:
  - Every element enters with a spring and leaves with intent. Nothing just fades in.
  - Kinetic headlines carry the one word that matters in colour.
  - Camera pushes, iris wipes between acts, screen shake on stamps, confetti on a kept
    promise, fire on a broken one.
- Captions burned in, word by word, with the current word lit. The film works with the sound
  off.

## Sound

- A bright, bouncy score in a major key, with bass, plucks, claps and a hat.
- It drops to a lonely tick in the "coming soon" act and builds back when KEPT arrives.
- Cartoon sound design: pops, boings, coin clinks, page flips, stamp thumps, a fire crackle and
  a sparkle on every win.
- Narration by one warm, quick voice.
- Mastered to -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP.

## Story (about 2:00)

| Act | Time | What we see | What we hear |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The hype | 0:00 | A token launches, a roadmap poster slams down, holders cheer | A new token, a big roadmap, everyone excited |
| 2. Day one | 0:10 | The team gets a giant stack of coins, locked by a calendar padlock | The team already holds a pile of tokens, vesting unlocks a little every month |
| 3. Coming soon | 0:24 | Calendar pages fly, coins pour out, the app stays an empty scaffold with a "SOON" sign, holders fall asleep | Vesting watches the calendar, never the work |
| 4. Why it matters | 0:42 | Two identical teams behind a curtain; one has a laptop, one has a megaphone; a trust meter drains | You can't tell builders from talkers, honest teams pay the price |
| 5. Meet KEPT | 0:56 | Iris wipe, the seal drops and bursts | What if tokens unlocked only when the promise was kept |
| 6. The vault | 1:02 | Coins drop into a vault with four milestone drawers | Lock tokens behind milestones anyone can check |
| 7. The swarm | 1:16 | Nine agents fly out, inspect, vote; a meter counts to 7 | Nine independent agents check, seven must agree |
| 8. The verdict | 1:32 | KEPT stamp, coins fly to a happy team; BROKEN stamp, coins burn | Kept goes to the team, broken burns |
| 9. It's real | 1:42 | The live numbers: 300M locked, 2 promises confirmed | Live on Robinhood Chain |
| 10. The end | 1:52 | "Don't trust the roadmap. Check it." The logo and the link | Promises the swarm enforces |

## Rules

- 1920x1080, 30 fps, at most 2:05.
- Every claim is true of the live product.
- No em dashes, no filler words, no stock phrases.
